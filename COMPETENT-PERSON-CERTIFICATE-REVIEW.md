## Mandatory cross-cutting decisions

1. Verify Paramount Fire Protection as contractor and draft issuer; distinguish
   issued-for branding, customer, engineer, approver and legal issuer. Approve
   Absolute name/artwork separately; no invented logo or delegated authority.
2. Check that incomplete/not tested/out-of-scope results cannot appear as a pass.
   Repeated services must reset readings, results, signatures and declarations.
   Check batch totals against every unique asset and preserve independent visits.
3. Check observations versus measured evidence, units, dates/time zones, limitations,
   accessibility constraints, defects, action ownership and follow-up. No default
   value may imply work was performed. Resolve any standards/declaration wording.
4. Inspect every mapped PDF field and Word placeholder; unsupported text, overflow,
   split placeholders and handwritten/manual fields require explicit completion.
   Compare original hashes and historical versions. Review long/multiple-page
   outputs and both brands in actual Word and the intended PDF viewers.
5. Review controlled issue separately from drafts. Confirm exact frozen revision,
   competent approver role/note, source version, change history, issue sequence and
   invalidation/correction procedure. New edits must not rewrite an issued record.
   Acknowledgement proves only the implemented mailbox challenge plus recorded
   self-declared name/authority and consent. It is not qualified identity or
   PAdES-LT evidence. Review signing key/certificate trust and evidence retention.
6. Review offline copies/exports, revocation limits, retention and lost-device
   procedure. Shared cached metadata/device clocks are not trusted authorisation.
   No customer issue should rely on an unchecked offline template or stale role.
